1. NET Cloudflare Inc.
4 个来源印证 🔥3-6 monthshigh
Cloudflare appears across four independent theses: (1) edge inference infrastructure for open-source/non-US model deployments as European enterprises hedge US model dependency; (2) cloud browser/remote computer infrastructure for AI agent workloads at consumer scale; (3) WAF/CDN as the documented 'first line of defense' during AI-accelerated exploit waves; and (4) default autonomous deployment backend for vibe-coded apps ('I've given it access to my Cloudflare account'). Each thesis is individually non-consensus; together they represent a convergent re-rating case the sell-side has not unified.
🔍 为何尚未被定价：Sell-side models Cloudflare primarily as an enterprise Zero Trust and CDN business. The agent-browser-as-a-service demand driver, the geopolitically-driven open-source model inference use case, and the prosumer vibe-coder deployment volume are each individually unmodeled and collectively represent a new, faster-growing demand layer outside consensus. The company is not cheap, but the TAM expansion from agentic workloads is not in anyone's numbers.

Cloudflare's zero-trust architecture and global edge are not replicable by hyperscalers at the same latency profile, meaning it is not a zero-sum competitor but a complementary layer they route through.
