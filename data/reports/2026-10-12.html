As AI chip packaging complexity increases with each NVIDIA/AMD/custom-silicon generation, ABF substrate demand compounds — this is a non-substitutable, patent-protected material with no credible near-term alternative.

The AI chip packaging angle is a second-order implication that most generalist investors miss because the brand name connotes seasoning, not semiconductors.

正方反驳 ↩︎：The ADR discount to the underlying and the near-total absence of English-language sell-side coverage specifically on ABF means the US price discovery mechanism is broken for this name.
