1. MU Micron Technology Inc.
4 个来源印证 🔥6-12 monthshigh

Micron is the primary US-listed pure-play on this bottleneck, yet the market still prices it as a cyclical commodity memory company. The 17x vs 120x bandwidth/FLOP divergence and 96% KV-cache hit-rate economics are not in consensus analyst models.
